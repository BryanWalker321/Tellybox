# Tellybox community code of conduct

## A welcoming community

Tellybox welcomes viewers, station owners and contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Treat people with respect regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, nationality, appearance, religion, sex, gender identity or sexual orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be considerate, patient and constructive. Critique ideas and code rather than people.
- Respect different experiences, accessibility needs and levels of technical knowledge.
- Accept reasonable moderation decisions and take responsibility when you cause harm.
- Respect privacy, consent, copyright and the rules of the platform you are using.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, personal attacks, doxxing, impersonation, deliberate disruption and repeated unwanted contact are not acceptable. Do not post another person's private information, credentials or private messages without permission. Do not use project spaces to promote abuse or evade moderation.

## Where this applies

This code applies to this repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, and to the official Tellybox community spaces. It also applies when someone represents the project publicly. Independently operated self-hosted stations are responsible for their own community rules and moderation.

## Reporting a concern

For a concern in the official community, [join the Tellybox Discord](https://discord.gg/WeTsEbdcCZ) and ask a moderator for a private conversation. Do not publish sensitive details in a public channel. If the concern involves a moderator, contact another moderator where possible.

On GitHub, use the content's **Report content** option for abuse or the account's **Report or block** controls. See [GitHub's reporting guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam). You do not need to confront the person involved. Security vulnerabilities use the separate [security reporting process](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers will consider reports fairly, limit disclosure of private details to what is needed to investigate or meet platform/legal requirements, and consider conflicts of interest. Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed on third-party platforms. Retaliation against someone making a good-faith report is unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content and issue a warning, restrict participation temporarily or exclude someone from project spaces. The response depends on severity, impact and any repeated behaviour; serious threats or harassment can lead to immediate exclusion. Maintainers may refer platform violations to GitHub or Discord. Someone affected by a decision may request a review through a private moderator conversation, preferably with a maintainer who was not involved in the original decision.

This is a project-specific code of conduct, maintained by the Tellybox maintainers.
