THIS IS AN INDEPENDENT PRODUCTION

Old television.
New possibilities.

A television service for the curious.

Tellybox brings British television preserved in the Internet Archive back to the channel dial. Computing, continuity, adventure and yesterday’s future. The first edition is a small pilot catalogue, ready to grow with careful curation.

It’s already on.

Every channel follows a repeating schedule anchored to a shared date. When you tune in, you join the programme in progress. Explore the newspaper-style guide to find your day’s viewing, then settle on a channel.

The credit belongs to the creators.

Media streams directly from its host. Source credits appear beside the programme on air. This station is independent of the Internet Archive, the BBC, ITV and other broadcasters.

Archive availability does not establish permission to rebroadcast or redistribute. Review both the material and its rights before operating a public station.
