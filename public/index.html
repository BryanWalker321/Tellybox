THIS IS AN INDEPENDENT PRODUCTION

Old television.
New possibilities.

A television service for the curious.

Tellybox brings British television preserved in the Internet Archive back to the channel dial. Computing, continuity, adventure and yesterday’s future. The first edition is a small pilot catalogue, ready to grow with careful curation.

It’s already on.

Every channel follows a repeating schedule anchored to a shared date. When you tune in, you join the programme in progress. Explore the newspaper-style guide to find your day’s viewing, then settle on a channel.

The credit belongs to the creators.

Media streams directly from its host. Source credits appear beside the programme on air. This station is independent of the Internet Archive, the BBC, ITV and other broadcasters.

Archive availability does not establish permission to rebroadcast or redistribute. Review both the material and its rights before operating a public station.

    YouTube authentication for IPTV

    Optional: let this server use a YouTube sign-in session when preparing IPTV streams. Website videos still play through YouTube’s own player. Viewers do not need your account.

    A session file grants access to your YouTube account. Use a separate account for this purpose. yt-dlp warns that automated account use can lead to temporary or permanent suspension. Authentication may help a sign-in challenge; it does not guarantee playback or remove upload restrictions.

    Checking private session status…

    Connect your session

    1. Use a private/incognito window on the server computer and sign into YouTube yourself. Do not enter your Google password into this website.
    2. Export only the youtube.com cookies in Netscape cookies.txt format. The official export guide ↗ lists compatible Chrome and Firefox extensions. Allow your chosen exporter in private browsing if needed.
    3. In that same private tab, open YouTube’s robots.txt ↗ by copying its address into the tab. Export there, then close the private window. Follow YouTube session guidance ↗ to reduce cookie rotation.
    4. Open these Settings at the server’s localhost address (for example http://127.0.0.1:4173), or your HTTPS station address, and upload the exported file below. Plain HTTP LAN uploads are blocked.
    5. Test one sample. After a successful check, test the remaining sources gradually. Replace the session if it expires.

    The session is stored in the server’s private data folder and is never returned to this page, the catalogue or source downloads. After uploading, delete your exported copy when it is no longer needed. Do not paste it into chat or upload it as programme media.

    Check an IPTV sample

    This checks real HLS programme footage and decodes a frame. It does not mark other videos as verified or change the schedule.

      Your Discord community

      Show Discord buttons on your station. Self-hosted owners should use their own community invite.

      Automatically join viewers after sign-in

      1. Use the same Discord application as sign-in. Open its Bot page and copy its bot token.
      2. Under OAuth2 → URL Generator, select the bot scope and Create Instant Invite permission. Open the generated installation URL and install the bot in your server. Administrator permission and privileged intents are not required.
      3. In Discord, turn on Developer Mode under Advanced settings. Right-click your server and Copy Server ID.
      4. Enter the server ID and bot token below, enable automatic joining and save. New sign-ins will request identify and guilds.join permission. Visitors must approve Discord’s authorisation screen.

      Leave blank to join without assigning a role. The bot needs Manage Roles permission and its highest role must be above this member role. This also gives the role to existing server members when they sign in again; other roles are preserved.

      The bot token stays private on your server. Viewer access tokens are used briefly to sign in and join, then discarded. Existing viewers must sign in again to authorise joining. Discord’s rules screening still applies.

      Log comments to your Discord server

      In your Discord server, open the destination text channel → Edit Channel → Integrations → Webhooks. Create a webhook and paste its URL here. All station channels are labelled in the log. No bot installation is required.

      Saved secrets stay on the server. Leave these fields blank to keep their current values.

      The webhook check reads its connection details; it does not post a message. Discord sign-in reads the viewer’s public profile. When automatic joining is enabled, it also requests permission to add them to your configured server. This is a website conversation logged to Discord, not a two-way Discord channel.
